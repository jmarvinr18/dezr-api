## Corporations

Our corporate team supports companies from incorporation through growth and restructuring:

- Company incorporation and registration
- Corporate secretarial and compliance support
- Trust, nominee and escrow arrangements
- Foreign investment rules and approvals
- Setting up branches, representative offices and regional headquarters
- Mergers, acquisitions and joint ventures
- Economic zone registration

## Banking & Finance

We act for lenders, issuers, underwriters and investors on:

- Issuing and listing securities
- Tender offers
- Due diligence for issuers and underwriters
- Broker and dealer regulation
- Project and real estate finance
- Bank regulatory matters
- Loan documentation and debt restructuring
- Foreign exchange and cross-border trade transactions
- Digital assets and cryptocurrency

## Trade & Commerce

We draft and negotiate the contracts businesses run on, including supply, service and franchise agreements. We also support projects in real estate, construction, telecoms, energy, utilities, infrastructure and mining.

## Taxation

Tax advice is part of all our commercial work. We help clients plan for and comply with withholding tax, VAT, documentary stamp tax, transfer taxes and customs duties, and we handle claims for tax-treaty relief.

## Intellectual Property

We register, license and enforce patents, trademarks and copyrights, and we structure technology transfers:

- Filing and registration
- Licensing agreements
- Infringement actions
- Unfair competition claims
- Inter partes and interference proceedings
- Seizure and enforcement
- E-commerce and online brand protection

## Immigration

We obtain resident visas and permits for:

- Executives and technical staff
- Investors
- Retirees

## Employment

We represent employers in collective bargaining. We also advise on everyday labor questions: company policies, contracting, hiring, termination, pay and benefits, and social security.

## Dispute Resolution

We litigate before the courts and administrative agencies in all of our practice areas. When a dispute is better settled out of court, we handle negotiation, mediation and arbitration.
